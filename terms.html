Gameplay

Durak is a 36-card game for two, three or four players. Each player plays individually. Offline games use computer opponents. The app does not offer real-money betting, prizes, in-app purchases or advertising.

Online play

Online play uses Apple Game Center and requires compatible app versions, matching table sizes, Game Center authentication and a network connection. Apple services are subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. Availability depends on those services and participants' connections. Online matches cannot currently be resumed after a participant disconnects.

Saved games and preferences

Offline saves and preferences are stored on your device. A new offline game replaces the previous offline save. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to device backup settings.
