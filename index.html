Durak — Support

Classic 36-card throw-in Durak for iPhone and iPad. Choose 2–4 players, play offline against computers, or use Apple Game Center for online play.

Language

Tap the globe icon on the home screen or during a game, then choose a language. System language follows your device settings. Explicit choices are remembered.

Computer difficulty

Choose Easy, Medium or Hard before starting an offline game. You can resume a saved offline game from the home screen.

Online play

Sign in to Game Center in device settings. Each participant needs a compatible app version, the same table size and a network connection. Invite friends or find players through the Game Center matchmaker. Online matches cannot be resumed after a player disconnects.
