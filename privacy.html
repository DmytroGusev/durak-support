Durak — Privacy Policy

Updated 29 October 2026. Developer: Dmytro Husiev.

Offline play

Saved games, language and difficulty preferences stay on your device, subject to your device backups. Offline play does not require our room service. There are no ads, analytics SDKs, purchases or real-money bets.

Game Center

Apple Game Center handles authentication, invitations and the game connection. Match participants receive Game Center player identifiers and display names, public table state and their own private hand. Our room service does not receive card hands or game moves. Earlier builds without the room directory use Game Center directly.

Public and private tables — builds 11 and later

The online table directory is operated by the developer on Cloudflare. When you use it, we verify an Apple-signed Game Center identity proof and temporarily process your Game Center team player identifier, session credentials, room membership, readiness and connection timestamps. No separate username or password is required.

The public list shows a room code, occupied and available seats and room status. It does not expose Game Center identifiers. A private table is omitted from the public list and requires its invitation link. After all participants are ready, room members receive the identifiers needed to verify their Game Center match.

Retention and security

Waiting rooms and inactive seats expire automatically; leaving a room removes membership or closes the table. Authentication sessions expire after about one hour. Short-lived request records and identity-proof replay checks expire after about two and five minutes respectively, followed by periodic cleanup. For abuse prevention the service temporarily stores a hash of the connecting IP address for about one minute, followed by cleanup. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Cloudflare processes hosting and network data to operate the service under its privacy policy. Apple processes Game Center data under Apple’s privacy policy.

Your choices

You can play offline, leave an online table, or stop using the room directory. Uninstalling removes local app data subject to your backups; temporary server records expire as described above. Contact us with privacy or deletion questions.
