Durak — Privacy Policy

Updated 14 October 2026. Developer: Dmytro Husiev.

Offline games

Your saved game, language preference and computer difficulty are stored on your device. They may be included in backups managed by your device settings. Durak has no developer-operated accounts, analytics, advertising, purchases or developer-operated game server.

Online games

Online play uses Apple Game Center. Apple handles Game Center authentication and matchmaking under its own privacy practices. During a match, Game Center player identifiers, display names and game actions are used to coordinate play. Participants see each other's Game Center display names and public table state; each player receives only their own private hand. We do not retain these match messages on a developer-operated server.

Read Apple's privacy policy and manage your Game Center account through device settings.

Your choices

You can play offline without signing in to Game Center. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings. Saved offline games are replaced when a new offline game is started. Leaving an online table ends the current match session.
